Phase 3 perception: RGB-D detection of bin parts (Gazebo, headless).
Recorded 2026-07-26. ROS 2 Jazzy, gz Sim 8.

    ros2 launch armik_moveit perception.launch.py

A downward RGB-D camera over the supply bin renders four coloured parts. The
detector (armik_moveit/detector.py) thresholds each colour in RGB, takes the
largest blob's centroid, samples the depth there, deprojects the pixel to the
camera optical frame, and transforms it to the world frame with the known camera
pose. Classical perception, no learned model.

Detections vs ground truth (world frame):
    red   : (0.411, -0.379, 0.120)  gt (0.41, -0.38)  err  2 mm
    yellow: (0.531, -0.260, 0.120)  gt (0.53, -0.26)  err  1 mm
    blue  : (0.422, -0.271, 0.120)  gt (0.41, -0.26)  err 16 mm
    green : (0.516, -0.366, 0.120)  gt (0.53, -0.38)  err 20 mm

All four parts recovered at 1-20 mm (part cube is 40 mm), well within grasp
tolerance. z = 0.12 is the part top surface (the depth camera sees the top).
Poses are published on /detected_parts (geometry_msgs/PoseArray).

Next: feed detected poses to the palletizer (replace the hard-coded bin cells),

--- perception-driven palletizing (closed loop) ---
Run perception.launch.py + ur5e_gripper_moveit.launch.py, then:
    PICK_SOURCE=perception ros2 run armik_moveit palletize

The palletizer subscribes to /detected_parts, transforms each world pose into
base_link (subtract the pedestal height, part centre + clearance), spawns a
collision object there, and picks it. The arm palletizes the poses the CAMERA
reported, not hard-coded cells:
    perceived 4 parts from the camera
    part_0..3 picked from detected bin poses -> pallet
    parts placed : 4/4    cycle time ~9.8 s/part    RESULT: PASS
